# Historical-reporting policy review wrapper

Risk: Tier 2, provider factual-answer adjudication. This source-only increment
is authorized within Session Prep operational-beta work for providers' own
visits. RN delegation is deferred.

## Change and boundary

When the existing server checks select the fixed value-free historical-record
projection, the arbiter asks whether that reporting policy conforms, rather
than wrapping it as a draft answer and asking for corrected medical wording.
The internal option is selected only by the orchestrator after complete
source-context checks; no browser metadata, public field or model selection
can independently enable it. The arbiter restricts the option to LOW
ENGAGEMENT. Validated excerpts still receive a LOW review floor.

The Magisterium system prompt and moral thresholds, mandatory reviewer call,
absence/refusal gates, strict duplicate-safe verdict parser, failure
presentation and canonical persistence remain unchanged. HIGH, moral,
synthesis, summary-only and other intent paths retain their existing review
wrapper. Selected quotes remain whole source sections with server-derived
dates and titles; a quotation does not establish current orders or a complete
answer. No retries, vendor/config/schema/Core/panel/authority changes occur.

## Evidence limits

The deployed predecessor passed five of six live synthetic questions. Its CBC
question returned the static required-review rejection, which can mean scope
refusal or a strict negative judgment. The actual private review assessment
and parser output were not retrieved. This prompt alignment is an engineering
improvement, not a proven explanation or resolution of that live failure.

Offline tests use complete clinical sections from earlier captured fictional
A/B/D notes and scripted reviewer assessments. They preserve the documented
proposed CBC order, later cancellation and missing-result text with their
source dates. These captures are not an exact current selector snapshot or
clinical ground truth, and mock acceptance does not prove vendor reliability.
